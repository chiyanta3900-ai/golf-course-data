gstyle.json is an adapted OSM database: green and fairway geometries are from
OpenStreetMap. Green polygon area centroids and manually matched hole numbers
have been added. The database is available under ODbL 1.0, separately from the
application code. The rebuilding script and source links accompany the project.

Background aerial photographs: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan.
https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html
https://maps.gsi.go.jp/help/use.html
Photographs are retrieved from the GSI tile service at runtime and overlaid
with estimated green centers and recorded shot positions.

ichikawa.json and mikazuki.json (2026-11-14, hole-level estimates):
The overview extents are adapted from OpenStreetMap under ODbL 1.0:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/216475592

Green centers, approximate outlines and display corridors were manually digitized
from GSI aerial tiles (WGS84 / Web Mercator), separately from the OSM boundaries.
地理院タイル（空中写真）を加工して作成。出典：国土地理院。
https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html#seamlessphoto
https://www.gsi.go.jp/kikakuchousei/kikakuchousei40182.html
Coordinates/outlines are estimates, not surveys or field-verified positions.
Display corridors are viewing aids, not surveyed fairway, hazard or OB boundaries.

Names, hole identities, pairing and par facts were cross-checked with official guides:
https://ichikawa-golf.com/course-guide/
https://booking.pacificgolf.co.jp/?cc_id=103&p=guide.course_layout
Official illustrations, photographs, text and third-party course databases are not
included in this feed. Reference images remain local research material only.
