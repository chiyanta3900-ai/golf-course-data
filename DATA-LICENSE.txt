gstyle.json is an adapted OSM database: green and fairway geometries are from
OpenStreetMap. Green polygon area centroids and manually matched hole numbers
have been added. The database is available under ODbL 1.0, separately from the
application code. The rebuilding script and source links accompany the project.

Revision 3 (reviewed 2026-11-15): visible green edges were manually retraced from
GSI aerial tiles (WGS84 / Web Mercator), separately from the OSM boundaries.
Green centers are polygon area centroids. Hand-drawn viewing extents include
nearby water and bunkers; the compatibility field named fairways stores these
viewing extents and is not an actual fairway boundary.
Revision 4 corrects approximate camera-start references and viewing routes,
including the mistaken 6H/11H tee-side relationship and 18H route at Mikazuki.
